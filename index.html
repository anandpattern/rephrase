What Rephrase does

One tap, any app

Works wherever you type: Messages, WhatsApp, Mail, Slack, Instagram, Notes and more. No copy and paste, no switching apps.

Fix grammar

Spelling, grammar and punctuation fixed instantly, without changing what you meant to say.

Change your tone

  • Formal — a polished work message
  • Friendly — warm and approachable
  • Confident — drop the "sorry", "just" and "maybe"
  • Polite — soften demands into requests
  • Casual — like a text to a friend
  • Shorter — cut the filler, keep the key points

Undo any time

Changed your mind? Tap Undo to get your original text back.

Uses Apple Intelligence

On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, Rephrase uses Apple's on-device model. Your text never leaves your iPhone, it works offline, and it costs nothing.

Optional: your own OpenAI key

No Apple Intelligence? Add your own OpenAI API key in the app and Rephrase uses OpenAI instead. Your key is stored only in your iPhone's Keychain.

A real keyboard

Full QWERTY keyboard with numbers, symbols, emoji, word suggestions, typo fixes, auto-capitals and double-space for a period.

Private by design

Rephrase reads only the text you ask it to fix. Nothing you type is stored or tracked. No account, no ads. Privacy policy

Set up in 30 seconds

  1. Open Settings › General › Keyboard › Keyboards › Add New Keyboard and choose Rephrase.
  2. Tap Rephrase and turn on Allow Full Access (needed only for OpenAI).
  3. In any app, tap the globe key, type or select text, and tap Fix grammar or a tone.
