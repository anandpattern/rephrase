Two ways to power it

Default

Uses Apple Intelligence

On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, Rephrase uses Apple's on-device model.

  • Your text never leaves your iPhone
  • Works offline
  • Costs nothing
  • No Full Access needed

Needs iPhone 15 Pro or newer on iOS 26, with Apple Intelligence turned on.

Optional

Your own OpenAI key

No Apple Intelligence? Add your own OpenAI API key in the app and Rephrase uses OpenAI instead.

  • Works on any iPhone with iOS 26
  • Key stored only in your iPhone's Keychain
  • Text goes straight to OpenAI — never to us
  • About $1 for 10,000 rewrites, billed by OpenAI

Needs Allow Full Access, so the keyboard can reach OpenAI.

Set up in 30 seconds

1

Open Settings › General › Keyboard › Keyboards › Add New Keyboard and choose Rephrase.

2

Tap Rephrase and turn on Allow Full Access (needed only for OpenAI).

3

In any app, tap the globe key, type or select text, and tap Fix grammar or a tone.

Questions

Is Rephrase free?

Yes. Rephrase is free, with no account and no ads. With Apple Intelligence it costs nothing to use. If you choose OpenAI, OpenAI bills your own account — about $1 for 10,000 rewrites with the default model.

Does Rephrase read everything I type?

No. It processes only the text you ask it to rewrite — the selected text, or the text around the cursor that iOS shares with the keyboard. Nothing is stored or sent to us. See the privacy policy.

Why does it ask for Full Access?

iOS asks keyboards for Full Access before they can use the network. Rephrase needs it only to reach OpenAI. Typing and Apple's on-device model work without it.

Which iPhones work?

Any iPhone on iOS 26 or later. Apple's on-device model needs an iPhone with Apple Intelligence (iPhone 15 Pro or newer) with Apple Intelligence turned on. On other iPhones, add your own OpenAI key.

Only part of my text changed. Why?

iOS shares only the text near the cursor with keyboards. Select the text you want to rewrite first, then tap a button.

Which languages does it support?

Rephrase has an English (US) keyboard layout and is made for writing in English.
